What we've learned

Four weeks of real replies sharpen the targeting and the message.

  • Direct-care providers respond best. Focus the list where the message lands.
  • Segment the message. Associations and providers hear different angles.
  • Reserve the replace angle for fits. Skip orgs that get their CRM for free.
  • Timing matters. Follow-ups are set for when people return.
