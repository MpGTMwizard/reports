What we've learned

Four weeks of real replies sharpen the targeting and the message. These are already being applied.

  • Direct-care providers respond best. We are focusing the list on practices and clinics that acquire and see patients, where the referral and re-engagement message lands.
  • Associations need their own voice. Membership organizations get member-engagement and renewals messaging, not patient acquisition.
  • Salesforce angle only where it fits. Some nonprofits receive Salesforce at no cost, so the replace-Salesforce angle is reserved for organizations that actually pay for it.
  • Timing matters in October. Conferences and leave are common this month, so follow-ups are scheduled to reach people as they return.

What's working

  • First interested lead. Kathleen at LearnWell Education, now scheduling.
  • Clean deliverability. 1.2% bounce across 5,290 sends.
  • Three CRM angles live. HubSpot, Salesforce and Pardot each with tailored messaging.
  • Nurse-founder voice. Lands well with clinical and operations buyers.

What to watch

  • Reply volume is early. 21 real replies so far; the curve builds as follow-ups land and the list grows.
  • October out-of-office. Many decision-makers are away; a second pass will catch returners.
  • Sharper offers coming. A free referral assessment and HIPAA audit should lift reply quality.
  • Room to scale. No-CRM and Marketo/MailChimp audiences will widen the top of funnel.
